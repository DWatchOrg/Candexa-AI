Privacy

Your data, handled with care.

This policy explains what personal data Candexa AI ("Candexa", "we", "us") collects when you use candexa.ai and the Candexa platform, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We comply with the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and its regulations (formerly the NDPR).

1. Who we are

Candexa is an AI-first evaluation and training platform for knowledge work. Companies use Candexa to build work simulations from their own processes, evaluate candidates and employees, and upskill their teams.

For personal data collected on this website and from our customers' account holders, Candexa is the data controller. You can reach us at geo@candexa.ai.

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist and demo requests: your name, work email, company, company size, the areas you are interested in, and the page you signed up from.
  • Customers: billing name, email, billing address and subscription details. Card details are entered directly with Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Meetings: when you book a demo, Calendly receives your name, email and the time you choose.
  • Evaluation and training data: when a customer invites a candidate or employee, we process their name, email, responses to simulations, scores, readiness reports and debrief notes on that customer's behalf.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and basic server logs, kept for security and to keep the service running.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to you: contacting you about a demo, early access or your account (our legitimate interests, or steps before entering a contract).
  • To provide the service: running evaluations, training and reports for our customers (performance of a contract).
  • To take payments and keep accounting records (contract and legal obligation).
  • To keep Candexa secure: preventing spam, fraud and abuse (legitimate interests).
  • Product updates: occasional emails about Candexa. You can opt out at any time with the link in each email or by writing to us (consent or legitimate interests).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use candidates' or employees' evaluation data to train third-party AI models.

4. Candidates and employees

When a company uses Candexa to evaluate or train you, that company is the data controller and Candexa acts as its data processor. Evaluation results are shared with the company that invited you. Candexa supports human decision-making; we do not make hiring or employment decisions about you. Questions about how a company uses your results should go to that company first, and we will help them respond.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers, each bound by a data processing agreement:

  • DigitalOcean: hosting and database (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Stripe: payments and subscription billing.
  • Resend: transactional email (EU region).
  • Calendly: demo scheduling.
  • Google Fonts: website typography (your browser requests font files from Google).

We may also disclose data where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Candexa, our customers or others.

6. International transfers

Our main infrastructure is in the European Union. Some providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent mechanisms recognised under the NDPA.

7. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist and demo request data: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer.
  • Customer and billing records: for the life of the account and up to 6 years after, for tax and accounting.
  • Evaluation and training data: as instructed by the customer, and deleted within 90 days of the end of their subscription.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), your local EU data protection authority or the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

To make a request, email geo@candexa.ai. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and our database only accepts connections from our own application servers. Access is limited to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users and regulators of any breach as required by law.

10. Cookies

candexa.ai does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it. Stripe and Calendly may set their own cookies on their pages when you check out or book a meeting.

11. Children

Candexa is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If the changes are significant, we will let customers know by email.
