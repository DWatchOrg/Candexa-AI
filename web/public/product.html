03 · Visibility and ROI

Turn AI fluency into return on your AI investment.

Start by seeing where you are now, then watch your organisation move up four stages: Readiness → Fluency → Expertise → Execution. The same evaluation runs across every team, so leaders see which stage each team has reached.

Candexa then connects that fluency to how work actually runs: where AI is speeding processes up, where it is causing rework, and what each AI credit is buying. The result is operational fluidity, with AI spend you can show converting into return.

How it works

Four steps. From first evaluation to a workforce that leads AI.

1Get readyEvery candidate and employee starts with the free AI-First Starter: a short, simple introduction to briefing AI, checking its work and keeping data safe. Everyone starts on a level field.
2Do real work with AIPeople work through a realistic AI workflow, with a loose brief and details that change along the way, just like real work.
3Get the debriefEach person receives an Agency Index score and a readiness level, with the evidence behind it. Hiring teams get tailored interview questions; employees get a personal practice plan.
4Improve and proveNew trainings arrive whenever AI or your own processes change, practice closes each person's gaps, and everyone re-measures to show progress. Leaders see readiness by team, where AI helps or hurts each role, and what AI spend is buying.
